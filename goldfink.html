Background of the business


Goldfink Berlin was an approximately of1910-1960existing company from Berlin with up to 8 branches located there, which were stationery stores (not manufacturers) with a focus on writing instruments.

The writing products sold werefountain pen,Stylographen, mechanical pencils, mechanical pencils, two-sided pencils and multi-color pencils.
