Multicolored fountain pens

Stand: 16.02.2026


There are not only multi-colored pencils that write with graphite leads, colored pencil leads or ballpoint pen leads.
Fountain pens with more than one writing color also exist- at least in the minds of inventors, because most of them were probably never made.

I have organized some patents chronologically and by number of nibs. But it's not all of them by a long shot! There are still hundreds of patents to be discovered, especially abroad.

Not all complymy definition of the mechanical multi-color pen, but they are still worth mentioning because of theCharacteristics of fountain pen systems:
  • It is possible to split a nib and supply a different ink to each part.
  • Or to feed an undivided nib with ink from one of several selectable ink reservoirs.
  • Others simply have several undivided nibs, each with its own ink container, although they differ in that whether feathers e.g. B. can be brought forward manually, or are permanently in the writing position
