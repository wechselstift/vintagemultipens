Nardi Tri-Kolor / Nardi Tri-Way

Nardi three-color pen

Stand: 28.02.2026


In 1918 Nardi filed the first patent and by 1924 he had perfected his "Tri-Kolor".(Like the "Norma Multikolor", "color" was intentionally spelled as "kolor" here.)
In 1945 he registered the trademark "Tri-Way".

After perfecting the mechanism, he also patented his invention in Great Britain and Germany.
In Camden, New Jersey in the USA he manufactured his extremely unusual three-color crayon.
It is one of the most recommended multi-color pencils. It is very old, combines various mechanical principles, and it isCan be completely dismantled without damage.

activity

In the rest position, the pin is fully extended/extended. Now turn the rear part by a multiple of 120°.
Then push the rear part forward until you hear it click into place. The pen is now ready to write.
To extend the colored pencil lead even further, turn the grooved cap. This relies on a gearbox and one with a bumper connected form-fitting connection starts a conventional rotary guide feed - but only for this mine!
To retract the Nardi Tri-Kolor, press the button on the side of the clip. The pen halves then snap apart again and This puts the pen in its rest position.
