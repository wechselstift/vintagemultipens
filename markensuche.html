About the edition

The DPMA has an archive of trademark sheets. But (to my knowledge) there is no search function.
Google Books does offer a search, but (I think) it doesn't support regular expressions and won't give you a single compact PDF does not find all search results (in my test).

If you give me your input via contact form or email, I will carry out the search and send you the result.

The trademark sheets are from 1950-2002, butstamps from before 1950 are also included.
This applies if they were resumed or subsequently published for the first time. That's why it's worth looking for older ones.

It's also fun to just scroll through an endless stream of creative images and text creations.
