Definition of a multicolor pencil

Because the topic is already big enough

1. The pen must have more than one writing color.

Shown: Single color pencil

2. It must be possible to consciously switch between the writing colors.

Pictured: Rainbow crayon

3. The removal of the lead or the use up of the writing material itself must account for what has been carried down.

Shown: LAMY Safari Twin EMR, digital pen

4. The writing leads must all be stored in the same writing body.

Shown: Pen collar

5. The writing leads must never leave the pen body during the color changing process.

Shown: Multi-pen container

6. If possible, all leads should come out of the same writing hole or at least the same side of the pen.

Shown: Two-sided pen

7. It should be possible to leave only one color in the writing position at a time and to use a designated actuation device on the pen for this purpose.

Shown: Rainbow colored pencil (with ordered colors), bundle lead ballpoint pen
